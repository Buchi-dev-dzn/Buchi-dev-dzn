## Other sources

`linkedin.svg`: LinkedIn “in” glyph from [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com) 6.7.2
(`svgs/brands/linkedin-in.svg`, © Fonticons, Inc.), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: recolored, scaled and
placed on a tile. LinkedIn is not in Simple Icons.

`portfolio.svg`: original globe glyph (generic, not a logo). Original work in this repository.

## Custom lettermarks (dashed border)

No freely licensed logo was available for these, so each is an original text-only tile,
not the official logo: `illustrator.svg` (Ai), `photoshop.svg` (Ps), `lightroom.svg` (Lr),
`premiere-pro.svg` (Pr), `after-effects.svg` (Ae), `csharp.svg` (C#), `lazygit.svg` (lg),
`pi-coding-agent.svg` (Pi), `orca.svg` (Or). Original work in this repository.

All tiles contain only `svg`, `title`, `rect`, `path` and `text` elements: no scripts,
links, external references or embedded images.
